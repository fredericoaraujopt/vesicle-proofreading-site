🔬 Vesicle Hunt

Help proofread synaptic vesicles in fly-brain EM. Pick a name — your progress is saved under it, so you can leave and come back any time.

How to annotate

  1. Click the centre of every synaptic vesicle — small round profiles (dark membrane ring, lighter inside). Click a dot again to remove it.
  2. Only the framed tile counts. You can pan/zoom out for context, but click inside the frame.
  3. Use Z+ / Z− to look at slices above/below to confirm faint vesicles. You annotate on the centre slice only.
  4. Mitochondria, ER and membranes are NOT vesicles — they are bigger and irregular. Don't click them.
  5. Press Snap back (or 0) any time to return to the default view.
